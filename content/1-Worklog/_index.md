**On this page**, you will find an overview of the weekly worklog entries during the **TAC@NABVN Starcamp - Product Data Engineer** internship program at NAB Innovation Centre Vietnam (from June 15, 2026 to August 14, 2026). Each weekly entry details the key objectives, planned tasks, and tangible achievements realized throughout the program.

The internship journey is structured into two main phases across 8 weeks (4 Sprints):
* **Phase 1 (Weeks 1 - 2):** Product Mindset & Discovery — Ideating and designing the **Financial Wellbeing Assistant** using Design Thinking, Customer Journey Mapping, and SDVF Frameworks.
* **Phase 2 (Weeks 3 - 8):** Data Engineering & Governance — Engineering the **Zero-Trust Banking Data Pipeline** on Databricks Delta Lake, covering Medallion Architecture, Data Quality Rules, ABAC/PII Governance, CI/CD, and System Observability.
