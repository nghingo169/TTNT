### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment at **NAB Innovation Centre Vietnam** is exceptionally open, professional, and collaborative[cite: 6]. Starcamp mentors, Product Owners, and senior engineers are always approachable and willing to support whenever I encounter technical or domain challenges. The workspace is modern and well-equipped, creating an inspiring atmosphere for both product ideation and heavy data engineering.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
My mentor provided invaluable guidance throughout both the **Product Discovery** and **Data Engineering** phases. Instead of giving direct answers, my mentor encouraged critical thinking—guiding me to navigate complex banking domain knowledge (e.g., APRA/CDR regulations), evaluate product viability via the SDVF framework, and troubleshoot PySpark transformations independently. The Starcamp administration team supported us seamlessly with access credentials, Confluence environments, and agile ceremony coordination.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The assignments directly aligned with my Computer Science background while pushing me far beyond textbook concepts. I had the opportunity to apply data modeling and SQL skills to enterprise-grade **Medallion Lakehouse Architectures**, master **PySpark on Databricks Delta Lake**, and implement real-world **Zero-Trust Security (ABAC & Dynamic PII Masking)**[cite: 1].

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The program provided a perfect balance between technical mastery and **Product Mindset**. I developed critical skills in Design Thinking, Customer Journey Mapping, "5 Whys" root-cause analysis, Data Contracts (YAML), Databricks Asset Bundles, GitHub Actions CI/CD, and professional presentation during Product Owner Design Critiques.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
NAB fosters an inclusive culture rooted in radical transparency, respect, and continuous learning. Working in Group `[TheHELLO]` felt like a true agile software team. During intense sprint deadlines, everyone collaborated, shared knowledge, and reviewed pull requests constructively.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The Starcamp program offers a competitive internship allowance, flexible hybrid working policies, and access to premium learning assets on Databricks, AWS, and internal NAB knowledge bases.

### Additional Questions

**What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
The most satisfying experience was witnessing the seamless bridge between **Product Discovery** and **Technical Execution**[cite: 1]. Starting with raw customer pain points for the **Financial Wellbeing Assistant**, conceptualizing user flows on Figma, and then engineering a production-ready **Zero-Trust Banking Data Pipeline** on Databricks to power those exact features was immensely rewarding. Successfully presenting our Final Showcase to NAB leadership and receiving positive feedback was a major highlight.

**What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**  
Including brief architectural alignment checkpoints at the end of Week 1—especially regarding complex Unity Catalog permissions and ABAC policies—would help catch minor setup confusion earlier. Additionally, holding more cross-group technical sharing sessions (where teams present their pipeline performance optimization tricks) would foster even deeper peer-to-peer learning.

**If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**  
Absolutely, 100%[cite: 6]. **TAC@NABVN Starcamp** is one of the most structured yet empowering data engineering programs available. It treats interns as real Product Data Engineers, offering genuine autonomy to architect data pipelines, enforce governance, and pitch product ideas while providing top-tier mentorship.

### Suggestions & Expectations

**Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
Organizing a mid-program "Product & Data Hackathon" or peer exchange session where different Starcamp groups demo their intermediate prototypes (like the Figma wireframes or Bronze/Silver pipelines) would encourage valuable cross-team knowledge sharing.

**Would you like to continue this program in the future?**  
Yes! I would be thrilled to continue my career at NAB Innovation Centre Vietnam as a Junior Product Data Engineer, contributing to large-scale cloud data platforms and eventually guiding future Starcamp cohorts.

**Any other comments (free sharing):**  
Overall, this 8-week internship gave me a clear, realistic picture of what enterprise Product Data Engineering looks like in a top-tier international bank. Sincere thanks to the TAC@NABVN team, my group `[TheHELLO]`, and my mentor for an unforgettable and transformative experience!
